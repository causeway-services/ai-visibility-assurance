## The question and the useful decision

**Site:** https://koios.news/ · **entity:** the Koios.News general-news publication published by the 561 Group.

**One realistic reader question:** “What is Koios.News, and who publishes it?”

**What the answer should get right:** Koios.News is a journalistic publication (not Koios Medical, Koios Intelligence, Koios beverages, or an unrelated Cardano-data project). Its own masthead names **The 561 Group** as publisher and describes its source-bound AI editorial practices. Source: [live masthead](https://koios.news/masthead/).

**The actual decision:** Do the available observations justify buying SEO/GEO help, and what is the least expensive defensible next move? One bounded search sample alone cannot establish lead losses, search-engine deindexing, AI model retrieval, or a necessary paid intervention.

## Three prioritized actions (and the stop decision)

**1. Verify the canonical entity carrier before editing it.** Have the existing newsroom/site Engineering owner inspect the actual emitted homepage `NewsMediaOrganization`/publisher identity, canonical URL, and relationships against the source manifest and current HTML. Where an identity field is genuinely missing, repair it in the *original publisher*, not on a new doorway site. Any `sameAs` relation must have authoritative first-party/third-party support; do not invent affiliations or social accounts. Independent recipient: the publication's authorized release and a public-source re-read.

**2. Reuse authoritative first-party clarification.** The current masthead already states the correct publisher, and editorial-standards/corrections links already exist. Ensure the publication's original entity/discovery graph and group-owned public identity point to those existing canonical facts where source authority permits. Do not generate repetitive “Koios” SEO pages, imply legal or editorial claims absent from the source, or conflate other entities sharing the name.

**3. Repeat a small, falsifiable panel *after* qualified source change.** The existing Koios query set includes “Koios News”, “What is Koios.News?”, disclosure, corrections, and editorial-positioning questions. Choose at most **three** real reader questions for the first snapshot, record the exact query, named public answer/search surface (at most **two** accessible surfaces), locale/context, UTC observation date, citations and factuality separately. Distinguish returned search excerpts from generated answers; record missing observations as unknown. Compare with the 2026-10-21 source observation without claiming causality from a small before/after sample.

**Recommendation now: do not buy a continuous GEO monitoring subscription or a $500 audit on this evidence.** Start with the first-party identity verification and the bounded follow-up panel. A small independent paid snapshot becomes defensible only if a real beneficiary identifies a consequential confusion that a free check cannot settle. This explicit **no-spend** result counts as successful independent advice.
