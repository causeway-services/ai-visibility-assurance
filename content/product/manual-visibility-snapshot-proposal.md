# AI Search Visibility Snapshot — introductory $195 proposal

**Internal, customer-ready quotation draft.** This is one proposed **manual service** within Causeway AI Visibility Assurance, *not* an enabled checkout or authority to solicit outside clients. The current mock-client launch gate remains closed. The dollar amount is a commercial **price hypothesis**, not measured profit or a published final rate.

## The offer in customer language

**Can people find the right information about your business when they ask AI-assisted search?**

For a proposed **$195 one-time fee**, we'll check one public website against the questions your potential customers are likely to ask. You'll receive a short, readable snapshot of what can be found, what looks confusing or incorrect, and the **three most practical next actions**. Sometimes the answer will be to do nothing, fix an ordinary website detail, or ask your existing SEO provider to make a small change. This is not a subscription or a promise to rank first.

**What we need to quote it:** your public website, your business/service area (if geography matters), and up to three actual customer questions. You do not need to grant private analytics or advertising-account access for this tier.

**What the initial service includes**

- Check public crawl and canonical/robots/sitemap signals where accessible, and identify obvious entity/contact misstatements
- Review **up to three buyer questions** using no more than **two accessible public search or answer surfaces**; label observations by source and observation date without pretending to see private model retrieval
- Note cited/mentioned pages and factual accuracy only when actually observable; distinguish a search listing from an AI answer
- Deliver a compact written finding, the three best corrections (or a reason to avoid spending), and one round of factual correction/clarification within seven days

**Delivery target to test:** within four business days of agreed scope and an authorized purchase. Manual delivery is intentional: the first buyers should receive useful work before we automate a larger service.

**Not included:** site changes, copywriting campaigns, guaranteed rankings/citations/leads, paid ad placements, private analytics setup, ongoing monitoring, exhaustive competitive research, unlimited query sampling or an implemented checkout. Each could be scoped separately if legitimately useful.

## Ordinary business decision

The first commercial question is simple: **Would a business with this problem pay $195 for that finite result?** An actual authorized conversation, requested quote or buyer reply can answer it. We do not need a completed 16-question study, perfect delivery-cost measurement, public x402 endpoint, observed ranking increase or investment-account source join before testing the price. Estimate work at no more than two ordinary analyst hours initially; if a real order later shows scope exceeds that capacity, negotiate or refuse before committing, rather than inventing a margin.

A qualified customer order must still identify the legitimate selling principal, total price/tax handling, scope/turnaround, refund and acceptance terms, and the actual authorized payment channel. Do not route money to an unrelated personal account or count a proposed price as an external receipt. Any net surplus available for Group investment is determined **later**, by the original CFO/treasury after real payment and operating obligations.
