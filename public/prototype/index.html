Before you spend money on GEO, find out whether you have a problem.

AI-search tools and consultants can give you dashboards, rankings, and recurring monitoring. The harder decision comes first: is AI-mediated discovery actually failing your business in a way worth paying to fix?

We are building an independent measurement service for that decision. It may tell you to buy an audit. It may tell you that a cheaper subscription already answers your question. It may tell you to do nothing.
